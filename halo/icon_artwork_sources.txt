Lucide device glyphs: lucide-static 1.17.0
https://unpkg.com/lucide-static@1.17.0/

Historical 56 masks: extracted from the central glyph region of the cleaned handoff
prototype/lab-assets.json at 16,20,24,32,40,48,64 px. Demonstration arcs and states
are excluded. Color masters: artwork/optical-master-{xbox,playstation}-color.png.
Xbox uses the canonical centered master (zero optical offset) on light taskbars.
Dark taskbars use xbox-color-dark.png, byte-identical to the historical master at
6795db2:artwork/tray/xbox-color.png, with -0.375,+0.125 logical px already baked in.
These two optical placements were retained after direct user feedback on both
contrasts. Runtime selects the prealigned master and applies no further offset.

artwork/tray/lucide-mic.svg; source:
https://unpkg.com/lucide-static@1.17.0/icons/mic.svg
SHA256: 05ea78b25f9ec6284612abe51f8a95f07c28659825409ed6a5cb286322efa117
Reproduce: tools/make_microphone_artwork.py, then tools/make_icon_artwork.py.
Uniform 0.5 logical px per SVG unit; stroke 1 logical px; visible-ink bounds
[4,2.5,12,13.5], centered at [8,8], no optical shift. Source conversion is a
development-only headless Chromium step; runtime uses only embedded alpha masks.
